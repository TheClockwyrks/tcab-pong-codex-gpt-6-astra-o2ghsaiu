# Carom

Two paddles. Two obstacles. A whole field of possibilities. Carom is a fast,
focused duel where a clean return is only the beginning. Take on the computer
in Solo or challenge a friend across a shared keyboard in Versus.

The first serve gives you a moment to find your footing. Then each paddle hit
pushes the pace higher. Meet the ball squarely to send it straight back, or
catch it at the edge to open up a sharper angle.

Move as you strike and the flight starts to curve. Spin fades as the ball
travels, so timing matters: a quick sweep can pull a return into an unexpected
line. The two amber obstacles create bank shots, sudden reversals, and chances
to turn a desperate save into an attack.

Keep your eye on the trail and stay ready for the next bounce. First to eleven
wins, but you need a two-point lead. At deuce, every return is another chance to
find the angle your opponent cannot reach.
